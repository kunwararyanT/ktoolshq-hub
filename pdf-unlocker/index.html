How do I remove the password from an HDFC bank statement?

HDFC bank statement PDFs are typically protected by a password consisting of your Customer ID. To permanently remove it, simply drag your HDFC statement into our tool, enter your Customer ID password once, and download the unlocked version for easy access.

What is the password for an SBI bank statement PDF?

SBI (State Bank of India) usually protects account statements with a password made of your Date of Birth and the last 4 digits of your registered mobile number (e.g., DDMMYY1234). Enter this into our local tool to strip the encryption instantly.

How to unlock ICICI or Axis Bank e-statements?

ICICI and Axis Bank usually use a combination of the first 4 letters of your name (in lowercase) followed by your Date of Birth (DDMM). Once you unlock the file using our utility, you can send it to your accountant or CA without needing to share your personal password.
