Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Exact KB Targeter work?

Our engine uses an in-browser binary search algorithm that tests multiple compression ratios in milliseconds, guaranteeing that your exported photo is just under your required limit (e.g. 20KB or 50KB) without manual trial and error.

Why is client-side image compression safer?

Traditional tools upload your private pictures, ID cards, and documents to their cloud servers. CompressCraft executes 100% inside your browser's RAM via the HTML5 Canvas API, meaning your images never touch the internet.

What format gives the smallest file size?

WebP consistently produces 25–35% smaller file sizes than standard JPEG at equivalent visual clarity. For government or portal forms requiring strict JPEG/JPG extensions, ensure the dropdown is set to JPEG mode.
