Frequently Asked Questions

How do I compress an image to exactly 50KB or 20KB?

Select 'Exact Target (KB)' mode, enter your desired limit (e.g. 50 or 20), and the engine will instantly optimize the quality and resolution to match your requirement.

Are my passport photos uploaded to a remote server?

No. Because these files often contain sensitive ID information, the tool runs 100% locally inside your web browser. Your photos never leave your device.

Which image formats are supported?

You can upload JPEG, JPG, PNG, and WebP images. You can export to WebP for maximum compression or JPEG for universal form compliance.
