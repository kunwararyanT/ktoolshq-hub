How the Gaming Deals Finder works

PC game discounts and free gaming offers in one place

The Gaming Deals & Freebies Finder combines two separate types of gaming discovery. The PC Game Deals tab uses CheapShark data to show listed offers from supported PC game stores. The Freebies & Giveaways tab uses GamerPower data to show currently listed game giveaways, in-game items, and beta opportunities across supported platforms.

The two sources are not the same kind of offer. A discounted game may still cost money, while a giveaway may have platform, account, region, time-limit, or eligibility conditions. Always check the provider or retailer before taking action.

Understanding game prices

CheapShark returns prices in US dollars (USD). The price displayed here is the value supplied by CheapShark, not a guaranteed checkout price for every country. Regional availability, taxes, currency conversion, store account settings, and offer changes can affect what a visitor sees at checkout. CheapShark deal listings can represent different stores for the same game, so compare the store and offer details before choosing one.

CheapShark says its store pricing is generally refreshed about once per hour, although update timing can vary. Offers can end or change between the time a result is displayed and the time the store is opened. Deal buttons use CheapShark's required deal redirect link.

Understanding giveaways

GamerPower lists gaming giveaways by platform and type. A listing can represent a full game, in-game loot, or a beta/playtest opportunity. Not every listing is a full game, and some offers may have limited availability or eligibility rules. The giveaway provider or participating platform controls the actual promotion, not K Tools HQ.

Giveaway listings are third-party data. K Tools HQ does not distribute game keys, guarantee that an offer remains active, or process claims. Follow the source link and read the terms shown by the giveaway provider.

Does this tool upload files?

This tool does not select or upload files. In the PC Deals tab, a game-title search and selected filters are sent to CheapShark to retrieve matching deals. In the Giveaways tab, platform, offer-type, and sorting filters are sent to GamerPower; the title search is applied in your browser to the returned list. The providers receive the requests and may process standard connection information such as your IP address. No K Tools HQ file-processing server or private API key is required.

Accuracy note: K Tools HQ is an independent discovery interface and is not the seller, giveaway organizer, or owner of the listed games. Prices, discounts, offer availability, regional access, and eligibility can change. Verify all important details on the linked provider page before buying or claiming.

Frequently asked questions

What the finder shows, where its information comes from, and what to verify before using an offer.

Is the Gaming Deals Finder free?

Yes. K Tools HQ does not charge to use this finder and it does not require a K Tools HQ account. A store or giveaway platform may have its own account, eligibility, or purchase requirements.

Are the prices shown in Indian rupees?

No. CheapShark supplies deal prices in US dollars (USD). The actual price and availability shown by a store can vary by country, currency, taxes, and account region.

Are all giveaway listings full games?

No. The giveaway source separates full games, in-game loot, and beta or playtest access. Check the offer type and the source page before claiming.

What does the CheapShark deal rating mean?

It is CheapShark's own 0–10 deal comparison score. It is not a customer review, product-quality guarantee, or promise that the listed price is the lowest available in every region.

Can an offer expire after it appears here?

Yes. Offers can change or end after the data is retrieved. Confirm the current price, availability, region, and eligibility on the retailer or giveaway page.

Does K Tools HQ sell games or provide keys?

No. K Tools HQ displays third-party offer information and links to external sources. Purchases, downloads, keys, and giveaway claims are handled by those providers.

Does the tool send my files to an API?

No files are used by this tool. CheapShark receives the game-title search and deal filters. GamerPower receives the giveaway platform, type, and sorting filters, while the giveaway title search is applied locally in your browser.
