Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Valorant sensitivity to CS2?

To convert your Valorant sensitivity to CS2 (Counter-Strike 2), you multiply your Valorant sensitivity by 3.18. This free tool calculates it instantly using pure engine Yaw math to ensure perfect accuracy.

How do I convert my sensitivity to Fortnite?

Fortnite uses a percentage-based slider which calculates differently than Source engine games. Our tool uses the exact 0.005555 Yaw multiplier to perfectly translate your current mouse sensitivity into your Fortnite slider percentage.

Can I convert sensitivity for Roblox and Minecraft?

Yes! While Roblox and Minecraft have unique sensitivity scaling algorithms, this tool applies mathematically accurate linear approximations so you can keep your aim and flick shots consistent across all titles.

What is cm/360 in gaming?

cm/360 is the physical distance in centimeters that you must move your mouse across your mousepad to perform a full 360-degree rotation in-game. Pro players use this metric to ensure their muscle memory is perfectly synced regardless of FOV or engine changes.
