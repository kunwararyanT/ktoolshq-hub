Frequently Asked Questions

Does this upload my photos to an external server?

No. EraseCraft uses WebAssembly to process the AI model entirely within your browser. Your images never leave your device, ensuring 100% privacy.

Why is this tool completely free?

Traditional background removers charge subscriptions to pay for cloud servers. Because this tool runs locally on your own CPU/GPU, there are no server costs, making it free for unlimited use.

Will my image quality or resolution be reduced?

No. Unlike freemium tools that compress outputs to 500x500 pixels, our client-side processor exports your image at its original, full-HD resolution.
