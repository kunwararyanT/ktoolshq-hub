⚡ Workflow: How to Trim, Edit & Burn In Subtitles

  1. Set Audio Language: Pick your spoken language (or leave on Auto-Detect) and choose between the Fast Engine or the High-Accuracy Engine.
  2. Import Video: Drop your vertical (9:16) MP4, MOV, or WebM video file into the local workspace.
  3. Trim & Color Grade: Jump to the Video Editor tab to crop the video length, boost audio, and adjust brightness/saturation.
  4. Choose Viral Presets & Pacing: Pick word pacing (e.g. 2-3 words), choose styles like Hormozi, MrBeast, or Neon, or tweak colors and strokes.
  5. Fine-Tune Timings & Spelling: Use the Edit Text tab to correct regional phrases or adjust the Global Sync Offset to align text to spoken words.
  6. Download or Export: Hit Render & Download Video to export a burned-in MP4/WebM video ready for Instagram and YouTube Shorts.
