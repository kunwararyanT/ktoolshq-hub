Check Caption Wording Before You Publish

This utility compares your caption, short-form video script, or on-screen text with a set of locally loaded dictionaries. When a configured term is found, it highlights the match and creates a replacement version that you can copy. It supports English, Hindi/Hinglish, and Spanish text.

What the Checker Can and Cannot Tell You

The checker is a text-screening tool. It does not connect to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or your account analytics, so it cannot verify an account-level shadowban, predict reach, or guarantee monetization. Platforms use broader policy, recommendation, and contextual signals than a simple word list. How to Use the Tool

  1. Choose the dictionary that matches your caption language.
  2. Paste your caption, script, or on-screen text into the input box.
  3. Review highlighted matches and the suggested replacement wording.
  4. Copy the sanitized text and make your final editorial decision before publishing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this detect a real shadowban?

No. It checks the text you provide against configured dictionaries. It does not inspect account analytics, profile visibility, recommendations, or platform enforcement status.

What languages are supported?

The current dictionaries cover English, Hindi/Hinglish, and Spanish. The matching rules are dictionary-based, so coverage is limited to the configured terms.

Will sanitized wording guarantee monetization?

No. Rewriting individual terms may change wording, but platform monetization and recommendation decisions depend on broader content, context, policies, and other signals.

Is the tool free and does it need an account?

Yes. K Tools HQ provides this utility free of charge without requiring a subscription or user account.
