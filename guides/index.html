Frequently asked questions

Are the guides free to read?

The guides are available to read without a paid subscription. Some linked third-party services or platforms may have their own terms, limits, or paid features.

Do I need technical experience?

Most guides are written as practical walkthroughs. Some topics include technical terms, but the steps and limitations are explained in context.

Are browser-based tools guaranteed to work on every device?

No. Browser features, memory, processing power, file size, and device compatibility can affect results. Check each tool's requirements and test with a non-sensitive sample file first.

How often are guides and third-party offers updated?

Guides may be revised as workflows and platform interfaces change. Live deal and giveaway details come from their respective providers and can change independently of this guide library.
