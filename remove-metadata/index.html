Strip GPS Location & Camera EXIF Data from Images

Protect your privacy by removing hidden camera EXIF metadata from photos before publishing them online. Every photo taken on a smartphone or digital camera embeds hidden technical metadata—including exact GPS coordinates, capture dates, device serial numbers, and camera settings. This utility strips sensitive metadata completely while preserving original visual image quality.

How It Works Client-Side

The image metadata engine parses the binary header blocks (EXIF, IPTC, XMP) of uploaded JPG, PNG, or WebP files, strips spatial and hardware records, and re-renders clean image data strictly in client memory.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about photo privacy and EXIF data.

What is EXIF data and why should I remove it?

When you take a photo with a smartphone or digital camera, hidden information (EXIF data) is attached to the image file. This data often includes your exact GPS coordinates (where you were standing), the date and time, and your camera model. Removing this data before posting online protects your personal privacy and location security.

Does this tool upload my photos to a server?

No. Unlike other metadata removers, our tool operates 100% offline using your browser's internal engine. Your photo is processed directly in your device's memory and is never transmitted over the internet, ensuring military-grade privacy.

Will this reduce the quality of my image?

Our canvas-rendering technique maintains the original visual quality of your photo while stripping away the invisible data payload. The output file is a high-quality, privacy-safe image ready for social media or sharing.

What sensitive information is hidden inside my photos?

EXIF metadata often contains your home GPS coordinates, exact time/date stamps, and phone device IDs.

Does removing metadata lower my image quality?

No. The pixel color data remains untouched; only the embedded background text headers are removed.

Are images processed on external servers?

No. Cleaning occurs entirely within local browser JavaScript.

Can I clean multiple photos at once?

Yes, batch client-side processing is fully supported.

Privacy Guarantee: All image processing occurs locally on your device via JavaScript HTML5 Canvas. We do not collect, upload, or store your photographs.
