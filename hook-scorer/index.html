Frequently Asked Questions

What is the optimal word length for a 3-second hook?

At standard speaking speeds (130 to 160 words per minute), a 3-second spoken hook should contain between 7 and 12 words. For on-screen text, keep it under 10 words so viewers can read it in a single glance without pausing.

How do power words increase video watch time?

Power words like "stop," "secret," "mistake," "truth," and "exposed" stimulate subconscious emotional processing in viewers, forcing them to hold attention long enough for the main content payload to deliver.
