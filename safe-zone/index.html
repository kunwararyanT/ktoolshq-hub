Frequently Asked Questions

How do I keep text safe on Instagram Reels?

To keep text safe on Instagram Reels, you must avoid placing important graphics in the bottom 20% of the screen (where captions appear) and the right 11% (where the like and share buttons are located).

Are TikTok and Reels safe zones the same?

No. TikTok's bottom UI 'danger zone' is significantly taller (blocking nearly 37% of the screen for captions and music tags) compared to Instagram Reels. You should check both before publishing cross-platform.
