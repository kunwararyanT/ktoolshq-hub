Last updated: July 2026

At KTools HQ (accessible from ktoolshq.com), the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information is received and collected by KTools HQ and how it is used.

1. Log Files

Like many other Web sites, KTools HQ makes use of log files. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks to analyze trends, administer the site, track user's movement around the site, and gather demographic information.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

KTools HQ uses cookies to store information about visitors preferences, record user-specific information on which pages the user access or visit, customize Web page content based on visitors browser type or other information that the visitor sends via their browser.

Google AdSense & Third-Party Vendors: Google, as a third party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on KTools HQ. Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google ad and content network privacy policy.

3. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our privacy policy and agree to its terms.
