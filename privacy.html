Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At K Tools HQ (accessible from ktoolshq.com), user privacy and data security are the foundational principles of our platform. This policy outlines how we handle data across all current and future web utilities hosted on our platform.

The Client-Side Guarantee

Virtually all utilities on K Tools HQ—including sensitive tools like the PDF Password Unlocker, Image EXIF Metadata Remover, and Video Safe Zone Visualizer—are engineered to run 100% client-side. This means the processing happens locally within your browser's memory. We do not upload, transmit, or store your files, documents, or images on our servers. Once you close the tab, the data is gone forever.

Analytics & Log Files

Like standard web platforms, K Tools HQ utilizes automated log files for security and performance monitoring (managed via our hosting provider, Vercel). This information includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, timestamps, and referring pages. This data is entirely anonymous, is not linked to personally identifiable information, and is used strictly to optimize tool routing and platform performance.

Google AdSense & Cookies

To keep K Tools HQ completely free, we use Google AdSense to display non-intrusive advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DART cookie) to serve ads based on your visit to K Tools HQ and other sites on the internet.

  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • We do not share any data processed through our utility tools with ad networks.

Consent & Future Updates

By using K Tools HQ, you consent to our Privacy Policy. As we expand our suite with new utilities for creators and gamers, this policy applies universally to all tools hosted on our root domain. We reserve the right to update this policy, and changes will be reflected with a revised "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
